# MARKETING

The marketing team deals with the presentation of the association to the general public. This presentation includes a wide range of activities to make the audience's reach as large and meaningful as possible. The marketing team runs social networks, maintains a website, creates photos and videos, prepares and plans ideas for events, creates various promotional materials, seeks sponsors and deals with public relations.
