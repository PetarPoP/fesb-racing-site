# POWERTRAIN

The task of the Powertrain team is to develop the vehicle propulsion system and all its subsystems for MotoStudent and Formula Student competitions. By simulating, optimizing and managing the engine and its subsystems, we get the most out of the engine. All this includes the development of a drivetrain that includes the selection of laptops, and the design and optimization of its components.

In the near future, the Powertrain team is expanding its horizons with the development of a battery pack and thermal management of the electric drive.
